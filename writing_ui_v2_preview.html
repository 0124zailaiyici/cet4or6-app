📋 题目
Directions: For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write a short essay on the topic 'Should College Students Take Part-time Jobs?'. You should write at least 120 words but no more than 180 words.
86 词 · 建议 120–180 词
📖 范文拆解
📌 第 1 段 · 引入观点
There is a heated debate on whether college students should take part-time jobs.
💡 开头句：There is a heated debate on whether… 引出争议话题
📌 第 2 段 · 正方观点
Some people argue that part-time jobs can help students gain practical experience and earn some money. By working, students can learn how to communicate with others and solve problems in real life.
💡 Some people argue that… 引出观点 · 连接词 By working 表示方式
📌 第 3 段 · 反方观点 + 个人立场
However, others believe that part-time jobs may distract students from their studies. If students spend too much time on work, their academic performance may suffer. In my opinion, students should take part-time jobs as long as they can balance work and study properly.
💡 However 转折 · In my opinion 给出个人观点 · as long as 条件句
